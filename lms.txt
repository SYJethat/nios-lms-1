Scope of Work for AI-Driven Learning Management System (LMS) for NIOS
1. Introduction
The National Institute of Open Schooling (NIOS) aims to develop an AI-driven Learning
Management System (LMS) to modernize its teaching, learning, assessment, and certification
ecosystem.
Aligned with NEP 2020, the LMS will provide an inclusive, adaptive, and intelligent learning
environment accessible across diverse linguistic and geographical contexts. It will integrate AI,
data analytics, and cloud technologies to enhance learning, teaching, and administration.
The LMS will serve as a centralized digital ecosystem enabling learners, teachers, and
administrators to create, manage, deliver, and track educational programs. It will seamlessly
integrate with national digital education platforms and comply with the highest standards of data
privacy, accessibility, and scalability.
2. Objectives
● Build an AI-enabled, adaptive, and inclusive open schooling platform for NIOS.
● Support personalised, competency-based, and self-paced learning experiences.
● Integrate with national educational platforms: DIKSHA, SWAYAM, ULLAS, ABC,
DigiLocker, and UDISE+.
● Ensure functionality in high and low bandwidth environments, including offline and lite
versions.
● Enable end-to-end learner journey from admission to certification with automated
tracking.
● Ensure data security, multilingual accessibility, and policy compliance.
